Troubleshooting
Vlan 40: Marketing
Problem: Unable to ping Vlan 40:Marketing 10.0.0.192/26 network from any device and vise versa
Test: 
Tracert to other PC IP address from PC9: No Information
Ping from other PC to 10.0.0.192/26 network and monitor via packet tracer simulation. 
Real cause: Vlan 40 was not yet created in SW1
Solution & Result: Created Vlan 40 in SW1 and was able ping to Vlan 40:Marketing 10.0.0.192/26 network and vise versa
